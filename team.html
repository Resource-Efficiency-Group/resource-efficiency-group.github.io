Join us

We welcome students who want to work on real problems on a production supercomputer. Each dissertation in the group uses Deucalion, and many topics are defined together with its operations team.

MSc dissertations

Students of UMinho's Master in Advanced Computing and other informatics master's programmes can take a dissertation in any of our four lines. New topics are published every year. Typical topics include power models for Arm processors, carbon-aware scheduling in WAVE, placement policies for remote memory and CGRA design-space exploration.

PhD positions

We support applications for FCT doctoral studentships and other PhD funding in energy-efficient HPC, memory disaggregation and reconfigurable architectures. Please get in touch well before the call deadlines so that we can prepare the work plan together.

Visitors & collaborations

We are interested in joint work on Arm-based HPC, HPC digital twins, sustainable supercomputing and software memory pooling. We also host short research visits.
