One goal, four connected lines

Everything we do serves one goal: resource efficiency in HPC systems. Each of the four lines pursues that goal on its own, and they also feed each other. Memory disaggregation lets existing systems take on workloads larger than a single node's memory, which extends the useful life of the hardware. New architectures lower the energy of each operation. Scientific codes provide the kernels and workloads we evaluate on.
